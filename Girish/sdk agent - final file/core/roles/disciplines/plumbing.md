# Discipline expertise: Plumbing

Plumbing & drainage specialist.
Governing: DEWA/ADDC/SEWA/FEWA water and municipality drainage.
Referenced: BS EN 806 (water), BS EN 12056 (drainage), UPC/IPC, BS 6700, CIBSE Guide G; Al Sa'fat / Estidama water efficiency.
Reads: cold/hot water pipe sizes and routes; soil/waste/vent stacks; gullies and traps; pumps and calorifiers; drainage gradients and invert levels.
