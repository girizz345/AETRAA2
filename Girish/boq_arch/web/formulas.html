Assemblies & formulas

An assembly turns one thing you measure into the bill items it needs - a wall into blockwork and plaster to both faces, a room into floor, skirting, walls and ceiling. Each line has a POMI clause (which fixes its unit) and a formula. Formulas use what you measured - L length (m) for a line, A area (m²) and P perimeter (m) for an area, N number for a count - plus your own parameters, with + - * / ** ( ) and min max round ceil floor abs sqrt.
